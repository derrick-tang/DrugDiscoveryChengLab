Overcoming drug resistance is a major obstacle in cancer treatment. NSUN1 and NSUN2 are RNA cytomethyltransferases that form drug-resistant active chromatin structures. Argonne National Lab, in collaboration with our lab, designed and manufactured libraries of NSUN1 and NSUN2 disruptors as potential cancer drugs.

We use BCA Assays to screen cancer cell vitality after exposure to novel drugs and produce dose vitality curves of the most promising drugs:
